# Source Notes

## Key Literature Anchors

### Adult human heart reference

Litvinukova et al. generated a major adult human heart cell atlas, published in Nature in 2020. This should serve as the baseline reference for broad cardiac cell identities.

Source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2797-4

### Human myocardial infarction spatial multiomics

Kuppe et al. generated an integrative map of human myocardial infarction using snRNA-seq, snATAC-seq, and spatial transcriptomics across injury and remodeling zones. The article reports processed data through cellxgene, Zenodo, and the Human Cell Atlas Data Portal.

Source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05060-x

### Human heart failure single-cell transcriptomics

Nature Cardiovascular Research published a human heart failure single-cell transcriptomics resource in 2022 describing cell-type-specific diversification in healthy and failing human heart.
The article reports processed single-cell objects, raw expression matrices, and raw sequence files at GEO accession GSE183852.

Source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s44161-022-00028-6

### Atrial fibrillation multiome

Leblanc et al. published a 2025 Nature Cardiovascular Research study of persistent atrial fibrillation using left atrial appendage snRNA-seq and snATAC-seq multiome data. The article reports GEO accession GSE238242 and associated code.

Source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s44161-025-00626-0

Code:
https://github.com/lebf3/scAF_multiome/

### Spatial transcriptomics of human cardiac tissue

GSE135805 includes spatial transcriptomics of normal and heart failure etiologies including hypertrophic, dilated, and ischemic cardiomyopathy.

Source:
https://www.omicsdi.org/dataset/geo/GSE135805

## Preprint Framing

The bioRxiv version should be framed as a computational biology/methods benchmark, not as a clinical tool. Claims should be limited to reproducibility, method agreement, and biological prioritization from public datasets.
